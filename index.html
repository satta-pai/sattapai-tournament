Privacy Policy

Satta Pai Esports respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

🔹 Information We Collect: When you register for a tournament, we collect your Google Email, Phone Number, City, Free Fire Game UID, and UPI Transaction details (UTR) for payment verification.

🔹 How We Use Your Data: Your details are strictly used for tournament organization, slot allotment, verifying payments, and sending prize money. We do not sell or share your data with third-party advertisers.

🔹 Data Security: All data is securely stored in Google Firebase. We use reCAPTCHA (App Check) and strict database rules to prevent unauthorized access, hacking, or bot spam.

🔹 Payment Info: We do not ask for your UPI PIN or bank passwords. We only verify the 12-digit Transaction ID (UTR) manually to confirm your entry fee.

🔹 Contact: For any privacy concerns, email us at satta0pai@gmail.com.
