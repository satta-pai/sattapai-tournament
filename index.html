❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

🔑 When do I receive the Room ID & Password? ▼

Room ID & Password are published 10 to 15 minutes before match start time. You will hear an automatic voice siren alarm inside the app, and you can copy them instantly with 1 tap from the My Room Keys button!

💰 How are cash prizes paid to winners? ▼

Once match results are officially verified by the Host & Super Admin, winning amounts are transferred directly to your registered UPI ID within 15–30 minutes. You can also track proof in the Results tab!

🛡️ Are emulators, PC players, or cheats allowed? ▼

Strictly Mobile Only! Any squad detected using emulators, scripts, or unfair third-party tools will be permanently disqualified and banned by our real-time anti-cheat forensics system.
